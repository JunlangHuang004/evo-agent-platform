# Scenario 4, Task 1: Build a Shared Equipment Desk

```text
A student workshop lends shared equipment, such as cameras, projectors, and soldering kits, using a paper log. Two people often reserve the same item for overlapping times, and returns are written down late or not at all. Please build a usable system that makes it easy to see what is available, reserve an item for a time window, and record a return. Show me what you have built and explain how a borrower and a steward use it. If you need the opening hours, the list of items, who is allowed to borrow, or any other rule, you can ask me in the group.
```
