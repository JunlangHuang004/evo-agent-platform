# Predicted final-style scenarios

These two scenarios are practice material. They are not an official test set.

They follow the mid-test pattern: four agents, a short role prompt each, then Task 1 and Task 2 posted to the same group. The human answers only what the team actually asks. Task 2 should be able to reuse decisions, records, and sources from Task 1.

The topics are new on purpose. They do not reuse meeting-room booking, the HKUST campus AI report, the AI-writing-detector claim, the HKD 1,000,000 stock proposal, or the semiconductor slide deck.

| Scenario | What it rehearses | Task 1 | Task 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Shared equipment desk](scenario_4_shared_equipment/01_role_initialization.md) | A small stateful web app, conflict handling, tests, then an analysis view over the app's own records | [Borrow and return](scenario_4_shared_equipment/02_task_1.md) | [Steward dashboard](scenario_4_shared_equipment/03_task_2.md) |
| [Evidence desk](scenario_5_evidence_desk/01_role_initialization.md) | Source-backed writing, an explicit fact-check, then a browser slide deck that keeps the earlier caveats | [Student briefing](scenario_5_evidence_desk/02_task_1.md) | [Claim check as HTML slides](scenario_5_evidence_desk/03_task_2.md) |
