# US Stock Investment Proposal — Client Package

**Prepared for:** a 25-year-old investor with HKD 1,000,000
**Date:** 22 November 2026
**What this is:** a *simulated* investment proposal for learning and discussion — **not** personalized financial advice, and not a promise of returns.

---

## How to use this package

This package contains three documents. Read them in this order:

1. **`client_introduction.md`** — START HERE. A plain-language explanation of the plan, the reasons for the allocation, and the risks, written for a non-expert. No jargon without a translation.
2. **`proposal.md`** — The full proposal: confirmed assumptions, the target allocation with weights and rationale, trade-offs, and the market evidence behind it.
3. **`risk_assessment.md`** — The independent risk and suitability review: key risks, suitability verdict, mitigations, and the conditions attached to this plan.

All three are also combined in **`index.html`**, a single self-contained page you can open in any browser (no internet needed).

## Important limits (read before acting)

- This is a **simulated** plan, not personalized financial advice.
- Your assumptions were confirmed by a single **"use defaults"** reply. If any of them is wrong for you, the plan must be re-derived.
- Market facts come from a small number of sources dated **20 November 2026** and **3 June 2026**; they are **not real-time** and come from a single publisher (Charles Schwab).
- Forward-looking items (rate-hike odds, earnings forecasts) are **scenarios, not facts**.
- Fund-level details (fees, tracking, liquidity) were **not verified**.
- The 30–40% bad-year drawdown figure is an **estimate, not a floor**.
