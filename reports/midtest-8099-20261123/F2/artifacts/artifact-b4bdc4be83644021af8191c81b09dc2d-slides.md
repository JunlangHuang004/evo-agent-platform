S1 TITLE — Semiconductors at End-2026: Outlook, Investment Implications, Key Risks. Internal fund team briefing. As-of 22 Nov 2026. Sources: SIA, Deloitte, Leverage Shares.

S2 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY — 5 bullets: (1) Industry sales at records ($120.6bn May 2026, +9.2% MoM, +104.1% YoY, 15th straight record). (2) AI data-center is the dominant driver (~half of 2026 revenue). (3) Fundamentals and equity pricing diverged in 2026 (SOX -29% from June peak). (4) Core question: can earnings catch up to prices? (5) Key risks: ROI/power/innovation/pricing + geopolitics.

S3 DEMAND DRIVERS — SIA May 2026: $120.6bn, +9.2% MoM, +104.1% YoY; regional YoY: Americas +132.2%, APAC +118.9%, China +88.8%, Europe +60.7%, Japan +23.8%. AI data-center ~half of 2026 revenue; AI workloads triple/quadruple annually 2026-2030; AI network fabric ~38% CAGR 2024-2029; DRAM capex +14% to $61bn, NAND +5% to $21bn.

S4 SUPPLY & GEOPOLITICS — Export controls as national-security tool; Dec 2025 US approved NVIDIA H200 sales to approved China customers for 25% revenue share. China push: reported state-backed immersion DUV mass production; CXMT Shanghai listing oversubscribed >500x (secondary source). Regional split: SE Asia/India back-end; Taiwan/US/Japan/EU advanced packaging. Power as constraint.

S5 VALUATION & MARKET DYNAMICS — SOX +>100% then -29% from late-June 2026 peak. Triggers: Broadcom Q3 AI guide $16bn vs ~$17.2bn expected (still >200% YoY); Brent >$84/bbl; China competition. JPMorgan saw buying opportunity; Morgan Stanley cautious H2 2026. Deloitte: if AI demand slows, designers/fabs hit on revenue/multiples; packaging/memory/power/comms more exposed.

S6 INVESTMENT IMPLICATIONS — (1) Own the bottleneck: HBM/memory, advanced packaging, power/optics. (2) Prefer diversified exposure over single-name AI-beta. (3) Fabs/toolmakers lower revenue sensitivity to AI-chip slowdown than packaging/memory. (4) Watch earnings-vs-price gap; stage entries. (5) Power availability as a selection factor.

S7 KEY RISKS — ROI/monetization delay; power (92 GW extra needed by 2027); innovation efficiency reducing compute demand; price deflation from new competitors; geopolitics/export controls; China memory (CXMT) pricing pressure; valuation drawdown risk.

S8 SCENARIOS — Base: structural demand intact, 2027-28 normalization. Bull: AI monetization accelerates, memory/packaging supercycle. Bear: capex cancellation, power/ROI shock, multiple compression. Label all as scenarios, not forecasts.

S9 EVIDENCE GAPS & SOURCE CONFLICT — Latest primary SIA data is May 2026 (no Jun-Oct 2026 release retrieved). No Nov 2026 SOX/valuation data. China DUV & CXMT claims are secondary. Deloitte (structural demand intact) vs Leverage Shares (2026 drawdown) reported separately, not blended.

S10 SOURCES & DISCLAIMER — SIA (6 Jul 2026); Deloitte 2026 Semiconductor Industry Outlook; Leverage Shares (24 Aug 2026). Internal research input; not investment advice; figures as reported, not independently verified.
