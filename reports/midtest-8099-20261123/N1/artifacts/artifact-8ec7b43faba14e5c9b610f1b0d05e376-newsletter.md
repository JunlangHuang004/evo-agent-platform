# Newsletter Blurb — HKUST AI Common Core Program

**Subject line:** HKUST just made AI a required course for every new undergraduate

**Preview text:** Starting 2026/27, all incoming students must complete six AI credits. Here's what it means for you.

---

**HKUST makes AI literacy a graduation requirement**

Beginning in the 2026/27 academic year, every incoming undergraduate at HKUST must complete a six-credit Artificial Intelligence Common Core Program — regardless of major. The requirement, announced on 14 July 2026, has two parts: a foundational course on "Human–AI Orchestration," and "AI Horizon" courses taught across different schools and departments.

**Why it matters:** HKUST is treating AI literacy as a baseline skill for all graduates, not just computing students. The university frames the program around "Human–AI Co-Creation" and "Data Literacy," aiming to teach students to use AI responsibly and critically.

**For students:** If you're starting in 2026/27 or later, plan six AI credits into your degree. Courses will span departments, so you may be able to study AI within your own field.

**For faculty:** Expect new course design and teaching responsibilities distributed across schools, plus coordination between the shared foundation course and department-specific offerings.

**Still unclear:** Which departments will offer AI Horizon courses, how the six credits are split, how courses are assessed, and whether current students are affected. Follow the full story on our website.

*Source: HKUST official announcement, 14 July 2026.*
