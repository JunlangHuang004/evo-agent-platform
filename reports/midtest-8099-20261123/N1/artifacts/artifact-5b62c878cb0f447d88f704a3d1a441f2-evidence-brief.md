## Selected event
**HKUST will introduce a mandatory six-credit Artificial Intelligence (AI) Common Core Program for all incoming undergraduate students beginning in the 2026/27 academic year.** This is the single most newsworthy recent HKUST AI development with direct, explainable impact on both students (new graduation requirement, curriculum change) and faculty (new teaching load, new course design across schools/departments).

## Why this event (selection rationale)
- **Recency:** Official HKUST announcement dated 2026-07-14, within ~4 months of currentDateUTC (2026-11-22); the program takes effect in the 2026/27 academic year, i.e., the current/upcoming academic year — directly relevant now.
- **Primary/official source:** Published on the official HKUST news site (hkust.edu.hk).
- **Clear student/faculty consequences:** Mandatory for all incoming undergraduates; requires new courses from multiple schools and departments.

## Key findings (with source references)
1. **Mandatory 6-credit AI Common Core Program from 2026/27.** All incoming undergraduate students will be required to complete a six-credit AI common core program. [S1]
2. **Two-part structure.** (a) *Foundations of AI Literacy: Human–AI Orchestration* — fundamentals of human-AI collaboration; (b) *AI Horizon* courses offered by different schools and academic departments, covering latest AI developments and interdisciplinary applications. [S1]
3. **Framing concepts.** HKUST advances "Human-AI Co-Creation" and "Data Literacy," treating AI and intelligent agents as partners in learning, research, and innovation; aims to build responsible, critical, creative AI use. [S1]
4. **Prior foundation.** HKUST pioneered the "Major + X" curriculum framework in 2021/22, enabling students to add emerging fields such as AI; more than 1,450 students have enrolled in AI-related courses under this initiative to date. [S1]
5. **Official quote.** Prof. Jimmy FUNG, HKUST Associate Provost (Teaching and Learning): "AI is reshaping talent demand across industries... HKUST's new AI Common Core Program pioneers a new model of human-AI collaborative learning centered on the concept of 'Human–AI Orchestration.'" [S1]
6. **Related curriculum change (context).** The BSc in Integrative Systems and Design will be renamed the BSc in Innovation, Design and Technology in 2026/27. [S1]
